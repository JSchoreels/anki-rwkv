Memory model · small vs big

How close is the small model to the big one?

Both models guess how likely you are to remember a card when it comes up (its retrievability). On half of all reviews, the two guesses differ by less than 1%. Large differences are rare.

Out of 100 reviews, how different are the two guesses?

    One dot = one review in a hundred. A difference of 1% means, for example, 85% for one model and 86% for the other.

    An example: the big model says 85%

    What the words mean

    Retrievability
    The chance you remember a card when it comes up, from 0% to 100%. Each model predicts it. Anki uses it to decide when to show the card again.
    Big model
    The current, larger memory model. It is used as the reference here.
    Small model
    A compact version trained to copy the big one, so it runs faster and uses less space.
    Curve vs instant
    The models make two kinds of prediction. The gaps are similar for both; use the buttons to switch.

    On average neither model leans one way. The small model's guess is only 0.1% higher than the big model's on average.

    A gap shows the two models disagree; it doesn't say which one is right. A separate check found the big model is usually closer to what really happened when they disagree.

    Based on 30.1 million reviews from 722 Anki users that neither model was trained on. Small model: the 8k model (SM55). Measured 23 October 2026.
