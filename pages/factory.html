Factory overview: what runs, what is planned, what is done

The whole anki-rwkv work in one place. The page updates itself from the job queue and the scoring files; takeaways and the backlog are edited by hand. Today it holds one track, the small model: a 331,511-parameter student (64 channels, distilled from the big model, the fork’s 2,762,884-parameter RWKV) trained on 5,000 dataset users and scored on users it never saw, full history, both models with int8 weights and int8 state. LogLoss is lower-is-better; the % is the gap to the big model on the same reviews (positive = student worse). Target: ≤ +1.0% on both heads over the 464 held-out users.
