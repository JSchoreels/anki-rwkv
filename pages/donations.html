User data collection campaign

This page counts the review histories that people have donated to us directly, after removing duplicates, and shows how far those donations are from three goals. Only totals are shown here: no names, no files and no individual review counts.

How duplicates and same-donor files are found

People often send the same collection twice, or send several files from one Anki profile. We merge these so that every donor counts once and every review counts once. Five checks are used, from the strictest to the loosest:

  1. Identical file. Two files with the same content hash are the same upload, and the second one is dropped.
  2. Identical reviews. Two collections whose sets of review ids are exactly the same are the same collection exported twice, and the second one is dropped. A review id is the millisecond at which the review was made, so two different people practically never share one.
  3. Shared reviews. When two collections share 10% or more of the review ids of the smaller one, they come from the same person, for example an older and a newer backup of one profile. They are merged into one donor and each review is counted once.
  4. Same study sessions. Deck exports from one profile can have no review ids in common. When two collections have reviews in the same 10-minute windows for at least half of the smaller one’s windows, they are treated as one person studying different decks in the same sessions.
  5. Same archive. Files that arrived together in one archive were sent by one donor and are merged.

Collections without review history, such as shared decks exported without their scheduling, are kept out of the review counts because a model cannot learn from them.

How to donate

To donate your Anki review history, ask the maintainer. A full collection export (a .colpkg file) with scheduling information is the most useful, because it holds every review you have made. Donated files are kept private: only the totals on this page are published.
