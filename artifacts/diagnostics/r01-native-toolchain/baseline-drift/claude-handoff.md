## What remains unresolved

The 7.261-second training gap has no established cause. Queue wait rose by 0.433s and measured GC by 0.030s; neither explains most of it. Worker preparation wall times overlap and must not be subtracted from serial training time. GPU copy event spans rose by 0.829s, but include dispatch/idle effects and are not pure transfer bandwidth. Observed training GPU utilization fell 66.54%→60.94% and worker-pool CPU rose 53.23%→62.27%; sampled windows are shorter than the general 120-second resource-assessment minimum, so these are descriptive clues, not causal conclusions.

Source changes add per-update CUDA timing events and elapsed-time reads, disabled experimental/native branches, and extra GPU clock/pstate/reason fields in the existing telemetry query. An extra telemetry process invocation was not established. The thread-limit helper already existed in historical source, and the main/worker/evaluation thread assignments inspected are unchanged; a thread-setting change is unproven. Historical full thread environment and GPU clock samples are incomplete. The successfully captured B37/T256 backward kernel's 18.176Kbyte/block includes 17.152 static plus 1.024 driver-reserved shared memory; it does not establish a static shared-memory regression. That small captured shape also cannot explain production large-shape recurrence stalls.
